The research · Snapshot · June 2026

The tourist trap costs 85 cents

What 32,000 menu prices say about who really overcharges in Amsterdam, and for what.

A study of roughly 900 Amsterdam venues, about 4 in 10 of the city's restaurants, built from their public menus. The essay carries the argument; eight technical dossiers carry the methods and evidence.

The spin-off · Snapshot · June 2026

The dish price atlas

Every reference table I build in an engagement takes this shape. The atlas is the public one: pick a dish, get the typical price and the range where 9 out of 10 land; rare dishes get a sensible range too, borrowed from similar dishes rather than made up. Not an argument to read: a reference to use.

the top band is where 9 in 10 of all 32,405 prices land · the bottom is one atlas cell, carpaccio, from 200 dishes at 172 venues · both are 90% bands
